HEALTH TESTING

What we test, and why

Every dog in our program is AKC registered with completed genetic testing. Many have had hips and elbows evaluated by the OFA, are cleared by parentage, or are currently under evaluation. We prioritize health, temperament and structure in every breeding decision we make.
