How it started

We've been breeding for over six years, and our journey began in 2019 when we brought home our first Labrador, Gracie. It didn't take long to realize just how special the breed is.

What started with Gracie and Duke has grown into a thoughtfully developed program built on love, care and intention. Today our Labrador family includes Mila, Moose, Bailey, Sir Knox, Šani and Lana, with Willow and Noland coming to us from Poland. Gracie and Duke, now retired, will always hold a special place in our hearts.

All of our dogs are AKC registered and genetically tested. Many have had hips and elbows evaluated by the OFA, are cleared by parentage, or are currently undergoing evaluation. We prioritize health, temperament and structure in every breeding decision we make.

Each of our dogs is raised around our children and their friends, which is how we nurture confident, well-socialized puppies. We feel truly blessed to do what we do, and we give thanks to God for the opportunity.

Giving back

With each litter we donate a portion of the proceeds to charities close to our hearts. It's our way of sharing the joy these dogs bring to us.

If you're looking for a well-bred, family-raised English Labrador with an exceptional temperament and conformation, we would love to hear from you.
