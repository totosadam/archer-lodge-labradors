CO-BREEDING

A small circle of partner families

We place a limited number of pups through co-breeding partnerships with families we know and trust. It lets a dog live a full family life while staying part of a thoughtful program.

Full AKC registration is offered only on a limited basis — we protect our bloodlines and the breed, following the guidance of our mentors in this.
