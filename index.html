HOW IT WORKS

Bringing home an Archer Lodge pup

STEP 01

Reach out

Call or text us with a little about your family and what you're hoping for in a Labrador. There's no cost to start the conversation.

STEP 02

Reserve your spot

A deposit holds your place on the waiting list for a current or upcoming litter.

STEP 03

Meet and match

As the puppies grow we watch their temperaments closely and help you choose the one who fits your home, needs and preferences.

STEP 04

Welcome home

Pick up in Archer Lodge, or let us arrange ground or air transport. We stay a phone call away for the rest of your dog’s life.
