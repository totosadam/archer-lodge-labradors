GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Prices, paperwork, travel and timing — the things families ask us most. If your question isn't here, call or text and we'll answer it straight.

How much are your puppies?
Puppies run $3,500 to $5,500 depending on the dam's and stud's pedigree — the full breakdown is on the pricing page.
What do your puppies come with?
AKC registration, a microchip, deworming, and their first set of shots. Every pup also goes home with a toy and a blanket that smells like their litter.
Do you offer full registration?
Only on a limited basis. We protect our bloodlines and the breed, following the guidance of our mentors in this.
How do I reserve a puppy?
Call or text (919) 899-8917, or email us, and tell us a little about your family and what you're hoping for. A deposit holds your spot. Our waiting list fills quickly, so the earlier you reach out the better.
Can you get a puppy to us out of state?
Yes. Ground transport is $2 per mile round trip from us, and air transport runs $850 to $2,000 depending on the flight and distance. We'll talk it through and plan whatever is safest for your puppy.
What health testing do the parents have?
All of our dogs are AKC registered with completed genetic testing. Many have had hips and elbows evaluated by the OFA, are cleared by parentage, or are currently under evaluation. Genetics and pedigrees are available on request.
Where are you located?
Archer Lodge, North Carolina — in Johnston County, just east of Raleigh. Families visit us from across the Triangle and from out of state; visits are by appointment.
Are your dogs English or American type?
English. Blocky heads, thick coats and calm temperaments, including imports from our trusted partners in Serbia and Poland.
Do you offer stud service?
Yes, to approved females with AKC registration and completed genetic testing. See the stud service page for what we ask and who's available.
